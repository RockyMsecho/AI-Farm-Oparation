Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what AI Farm collects when you register, sign in, and use the dashboard, and why. It applies to this application only - it doesn't cover any other systems your farm might use.

What we collect

Account and farm information you provide directly:

  • Full name, email address, and mobile number
  • Your password, stored only as a salted hash - never in plain text, and never visible to anyone, including administrators
  • Farm details: name, location, type, size, and primary crop
  • Your role (Owner, Manager, Worker, Agronomist, or Technician), which determines what you can see and do in the dashboard

Information generated by using the dashboard:

  • Shift clock-in and clock-out times, used to calculate hours worked and build attendance reports
  • Field reports you submit, along with any recommendation and approval decision made on them
  • Basic account activity needed to keep you signed in securely (an authentication token stored in your browser)

Why we collect it

  • To operate your account and your farm's data under a single, secured farm profile
  • To enforce role-based access - so, for example, a Worker's account genuinely cannot view or change what an Owner's can
  • To generate the shift, attendance, and field report records your farm uses for its own operations

Who can see your data

Everything you enter is scoped to your farm. Accounts on a different farm cannot see your users, reports, or shift records - this is enforced by the backend on every request, not just hidden in the interface. Within your own farm, what a person can see depends on their role: Owners and Managers have the broadest access; Workers, Agronomists, and Technicians see what their role needs and no more.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties
  • We don't use your data for advertising
  • We don't store payment information anywhere in this application

Your rights

You can view and update your account details from the Settings tab, and change your password there at any time. For anything this dashboard doesn't yet let you do yourself - correcting, exporting, or deleting your data - contact your farm's Owner or Manager, since they administer accounts on this system.

Note for whoever deploys this: this is a starting template, not legal advice. Before using this with real users, have it reviewed against whatever privacy law actually applies to you - South Africa's POPIA, the EU/UK's GDPR, or your local equivalent - and fill in a real contact point for privacy requests. The generic language above describes what the application technically does today; it doesn't replace a proper legal review.
